Privacy Policy: Sleep Pull

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Who uses it. This app is used only by its owner, to read the owner's own data. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it reads. With the owner's consent, read-only access to sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, activity and body measurement data from the Google Health API.

How the data is used. The data is saved to the owner's own computers and private notes for personal health tracking. It is not sold, shared, shown to third parties or used for advertising.

Google API data. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
